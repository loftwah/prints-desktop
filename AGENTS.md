## Portfolio design bar

Apply the [portfolio design direction](https://github.com/loftwah/app-composer/blob/main/docs/visual-direction.md#portfolio-design-bar) and [owner brief](https://github.com/loftwah/app-composer/issues/1422) to anything a user sees, reads or touches. Start from a sharp, modern, bright, playful direction. Use confident, vivid colour and make the first serious proposal bold enough to dial back. Compare several successful external products at the same task and inspect their real output. Treat current work as context, and keep the product’s own identity. The highest expressive option still has to meet readability, keyboard, privacy and task needs.

Judge the real artwork selection, comparison and export journey in the native app. Keep the work itself prominent and verify the controls against representative archive art. [Local follow-through](https://github.com/loftwah/prints-desktop/issues/4).

## Ambitious creator outcomes

Apply the [shared ambition guidance](https://github.com/loftwah/app-composer/blob/main/docs/ambitious-outcomes.md) through [#20](https://github.com/loftwah/prints-desktop/issues/20). Start from the creator's intended result, relevant saved decisions and current source. Use actual original artwork, precise native inspection and useful creative or engineering methods. Reuse the supported workspace commands and capability discovery so a chosen agent can carry the same brief, references and decisions through the real workflow.

For the feedback work in [#25](https://github.com/loftwah/prints-desktop/issues/25), show that a retained selection or rejection reason changes the next selection or revision after reopening or handoff, and finish with an inspected result and verified original export. Preserve exact human feedback separately from agent inference. Native creation remains subject to [#6](https://github.com/loftwah/prints-desktop/issues/6)'s actual supported contract; complete useful independent work through existing routes.

Use the smallest evidence that proves the changed outcome and record it in [#4](https://github.com/loftwah/prints-desktop/issues/4). Distinguish source, fixture, packaged-tool, native-platform and provider evidence. Keep at most two implementation units and one routine **120-second first-edit-to-verified-package/distribution budget**. Record overruns and the bottleneck, then continue safe resolution. Docs-only work finishes on main with its focused check. Preserve existing scope, spending, explicit holds and release safeguards; this guidance adds no recurring process or release gate. This local summary remains usable without fetching the shared reference.
