# Product

Prints Desktop is an independent native creative workspace for existing Prints
users and people curious about creating and keeping their own media. Its first
launch opens the offline Loftwah Creative Archive so people can inspect,
shortlist and export published portfolio work before connecting any service.
Afterlight remains a separate offline collection.

## Platform and stack

Desktop. Native Rust and Anomaly's Rune, with Apple Silicon first and Windows
and Linux intended. App Composer provides discovery and education. Oyster,
Prints and Darkroom retain authority over hosted assets, models, jobs and
billing. A compatible scoped connection is required for remote work.

Source of truth: [App Composer #1292](https://github.com/loftwah/app-composer/issues/1292).

## What we are building towards

The workspace should feel at home on macOS, Windows and Linux. Give the
artwork the screen: fast contact sheets, generous previews, side-by-side
comparison, keyboard control, drag and drop, and a layout that can change with
the work. Treat light and dark appearance, large libraries, file recovery and
accessible native interactions as part of the product.

A creator should be able to start with a reference and carry an idea through
experiments, drafts and finished work. Keep the original media, references,
model, settings and edit history together. Build towards image editing,
upscaling, background removal, animation, video and audio through supported
capabilities. Collections, tags, boards and reusable prompts should help a
creator resume the work months later. Codex, OpenCode, Shiitake and other agents
should be able to use the creator's chosen tools and scoped access. The
creator decides what to keep, refine or publish.

The offline first launch is a curated Loftwah exhibition. Use released work
from our games, records, videos and other projects and show the decisions
that turned it into something worth publishing. Credit the work and preserve
the exact originals. Add new collections when they have a reason to exist,
including original work inspired by Fungi, Oyster, Shiitake and Cloudflare.
Judge the archive on authorship, provenance and whether we would be proud to
publish the work on its own.

Keep exploration free and useful offline. Offer versioned downloads and source
without a registration step. People can connect their own Prints installation
and take their work with them. For connected creation, show the expected cost,
retain job identity and allow recovery without starting a second paid job.
Oyster, Prints and Darkroom remain responsible for connected work. Media Studio
continues as its own creative application.

App Composer is where people can discover the project, see what the native app
does, follow a practical tutorial and find the work in Share. Build the public
story with actual captures, finished work, clear attribution and specific
setup instructions. Show what Fungi and Rune contribute and how people can run
their own setup on Cloudflare. The [current preview](https://prints.loftwah.com/)
shows what is available now. The [flagship benchmark](https://github.com/loftwah/prints-desktop/issues/4)
tracks the broader work against real creator journeys.
